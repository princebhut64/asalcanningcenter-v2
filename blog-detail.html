How to Store & Can Alphonso Mango Pulp for 12+ Months

By Jalpa G. Patel August 15, 2026 5 Min Read

Mangoes are known as the King of Fruits in India, and the Alphonso (Hapus) variety is cherished worldwide for its rich golden texture, natural sweetness, and heavenly aroma. However, fresh Alphonso mangoes have a very short seasonal window. At Asal Canning Center in Paldi, Ahmedabad, we have perfected the cottage canning process over 24 years to preserve mango pulp for 12 to 24 months without adding harmful artificial preservatives.

1. Selecting the Perfect Ripeness

The foundation of long-lasting mango pulp begins with fruit selection. Mangoes should be fully tree-ripened with a firm skin and rich fragrance. Avoid overripe fruits with soft spots or underripe fruits with excess starch, as this affects pH balance and shelf stability.

"Pure food preservation is about respecting nature's timing. Through controlled thermal sterilization, we lock in freshness without chemical intervention." — Jalpa G. Patel, Founder

2. Thermal Pasteurized Seaming

After destoning and fine pulping, the mango pulp undergoes controlled indirect heating to achieve commercial sterility. It is immediately filled into food-grade lacquered tin cans or multi-layer retort pouches, followed by vacuum double-seaming.

3. Storage & Domestic Usage

Unopened sealed cans can be stored at ambient room temperature in a cool, dry place. Once opened, transfer unused pulp to a glass or ceramic container and refrigerate for up to 5 days.
