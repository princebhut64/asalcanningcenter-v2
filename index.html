Heritage & Philosophy

Empowering Communities Through Pure Food Preservation

Founded on September 18, 2000 in Paldi, Ahmedabad, Asal Canning Center started with a singular vision: bringing industrial-grade sterile canning technology to cottage creators and agricultural households.

Over the past 24+ years, we have specialized in Alphonso & Kesar mango pulp extraction, concentrated tomato paste batching, amla gooseberry cold press extracts, and custom tin & pouch seaming.

Our 4-Step Hygiene Preservation Process

From orchard selection to airtight thermal sealing, we maintain absolute quality at every stage.

01

Fruit Selection

Inspecting ripe, farm-fresh produce for sweetness, acidity balance, and natural purity.

02

Pulp Extraction

Hygienic destoning and fine pulping using advanced food-grade cottage equipment.

03

Thermal Sterilization

Controlled batch heating to eliminate microorganisms while preserving natural flavor and nutrients.

04

Tin & Pouch Sealing
