Standard Workflow

Our 4-Step Canning Process

Ensuring zero micro-flora activity and maximum natural nutrition retention.

01

Harvesting & Brix Inspection

Hand-picked farm fresh fruit selection evaluated for peak maturity and natural Brix sweetness.

02

Sterile Cold Pulping

Mechanical pulp extraction under sterile cold-process without added synthetic colors or water dilution.

03

Thermal Pasteurization

Controlled thermal processing eliminating microbial activity while preserving natural fruit aroma.

04

Hermetic Seaming

Vacuum double-seam tin capping or retort pouch sealing for long-term ambient shelf life.
