Brand History

24+ Years of Pioneering Food Preservation Excellence

Asal Canning Center was established on September 18, 2000, with a visionary goal: to bridge the gap between fresh agricultural harvest and long-term, pure natural preservation.

Based in Paldi, Ahmedabad, our center provides state-of-the-art pulp extraction, tin canning, and pouch packing facilities for individuals, farmers, and commercial brands alike. We ensure zero compromise on hygiene, taste, and thermal preservation standards.
