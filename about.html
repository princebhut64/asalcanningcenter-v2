About Asal Canning Center

Jalpa G. Patel is the founder of Asal Canning Center which is situated in Paldi area of Ahmedabad. Started on 18th September, 2000, Asal Canning Center was the first ever canning center established and led by a woman in the region.

Since then, the center has grown leap and bounds and has now become a renowned example in the cottage industries all over Gujarat. The organization takes pride in handling extraction and packing for Mango pulp, Tomato sauce, mixed fruit jams, squashes, pickles, and herbal health drinks.

Gujarat Chamber of Commerce Award (2007)

Honored for outstanding contributions to cottage food industries, extensively featured across leading newspapers, magazines, and TV channels.

“Mrs. Jalpaben believes that there is no other freedom like being ‘Independent’. Her dream is that every woman should stand on her own two feet.”

For this purpose, she continuously conducts seminars for fellow women to provide guidelines to start various cottage and home industries. Women interested in starting small-scale home/cottage food processing units may call directly for personal advice and mentoring.

International Orders: Asal Canning Center also accepts special processing orders for foreign clients. Email or call for comprehensive custom packaging parameters.
